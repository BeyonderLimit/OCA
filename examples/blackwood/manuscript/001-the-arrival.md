# The Arrival

The train left her at the valley platform with one suitcase and a letter she had not asked for. Elena Varga stood under the rusted awning and watched the smoke uncoil toward the hills.

Marcus was waiting by the gate, hat in his hands, wearing the patient look of a man who had rehearsed his welcome. "You look like your mother," he said, and she decided at once that she distrusted him.
