# The Return

On the last night Elena climbed to the kiln and waited for the moon to clear the ridge. What she found there was not evidence but memory: her mother, years ago, feeding a letter to the stove for reasons no one had ever explained.

The past, it turned out, had been waiting in the same forest the whole time — a decade before the trial, before the valley learned her name.
