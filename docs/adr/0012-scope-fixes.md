# ADR-0012: v1 scope fixes

- Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-08 · Code: **A12**
- Source: `OCA-architechture.md` Part II §23 A12 · rationale: `OCA-amendments.md` §1 A12

## Decision

- v1 story forms: **novel and short story** only (same hierarchy; a short story is one chapter or a few scenes).
- **Screenplay is out of v1** — Fountain adopted post-v1 (Phase 20) if wanted; format reserved via `project.yaml: kind`.
- **Album-level analysis** (thematic/emotional progression, callbacks, opener/closer) has an explicit home: Phase 10 (single-shot) and Phase 14 (album review pipeline).

## Consequences

- No screenplay code paths, formats, or diagram entries in v1.
- Album analysis is v1 work — do not defer it as "nice to have".
